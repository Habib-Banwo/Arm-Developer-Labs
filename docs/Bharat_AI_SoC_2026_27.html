BHARAT AI-SoC CHALLENGE

Bharat AI-SoC Challenge — 2026–27

2nd Edition

Applied AI on SoC

Participants Students & Working Professionals / Developers
Problem Statements 5
Format Three-Phase Innovation Pipeline

Overview

The inaugural Bharat AI-SoC Student Challenge attracted more than 3,000 students across 1,238 teams. Through three levels of evaluation and virtual mentorship, 150 shortlisted projects addressing five industry and societal problem statements were narrowed to five winners.

The 2nd Edition scales the challenge nationally, widens eligibility and introduces a multi-stage qualification process. Additional milestone-based grant support will help top teams progress beyond Proof of Concept (PoC) toward working prototypes and product readiness.

What’s New in the 2nd Edition

Two Participant Categories
Students (UG/PG) and Working Professionals / Developers compete on equal terms across five problem statements.
Three-Phase Innovation Pipeline
An approximately 8-week Open Qualifying phase, an approximately 6-week PoC Development phase, and a 6–12 month Product Development phase for winning teams.
One Winner per Problem Statement
Winners are selected after a live virtual demo and jury Q&A. Top performers advance to the Post-Challenge Product Development Grant.

Technology Theme: Applied AI on SoC

AI is moving from cloud-centric systems toward AI executed directly on System-on-Chips at the edge. This enables local processing, real-time response, privacy and operation without continuous connectivity, while meeting performance, power and cost constraints through the integration of AI software and semiconductor hardware.

The major shift in the 2nd Edition is from building AI applications to engineering AI-enabled products on SoCs.

The end-to-end engineering journey spans:

  • AI algorithm development
  • Optimisation
  • System integration
  • Edge deployment
  • Benchmarking
  • Validation
  • Product engineering

Three-Phase Innovation Pipeline

Phase 1 — Open Qualifying
Duration: ~8 weeks
Open qualifying stage where teams develop and submit their initial solution, report, code and demo.
Phase 2 — PoC Development
Duration: ~6 weeks
Shortlisted teams receive mentorship and develop a fully deployed Proof of Concept.
Phase 3 — Product Development
Duration: 6–12 months
Winning teams receive continued technical, financial and ecosystem support to mature their PoCs into working prototypes / deployable products and accelerate go-to-market.

Project Problem Statements

PS1 — Offline crop-disease/pest detection and advisory for farmers Theme: Agriculture & Food Security

Objective

Develop an AI-powered assistant that helps farmers identify crop diseases, detect pests and receive actionable advisory recommendations, running entirely on-device without internet connectivity.

Project Description

Students build an AI system capable of analysing crop images to:

  • Detect diseases and pests
  • Classify severity levels
  • Recommend treatment and intervention strategies
  • Provide region-specific advisory guidance
  • Support multiple local languages
  • Operate offline for rural communities with limited connectivity

Example Application Areas

  • Crop disease early warning
  • Pest identification and management
  • Fertiliser and irrigation advisory
  • Post-harvest loss reduction
  • Smallholder farmer decision support
  • Agri-extension worker tools

Hardware

Teams may use any Arm-based platform appropriate for their solution, including:

  • Arm-based Android smartphones
  • Raspberry Pi 5
  • Raspberry Pi AI Kit (Hailo AI Accelerator)
  • Arm-based Linux devices

Required Technologies

  • Llama
  • ExecuTorch

Optional Technologies

  • OpenCV
  • Hailo SDK
  • PlantVillage / Cassava / IP102 datasets
  • Speech processing frameworks for voice-based advisory
  • Multilingual NLP models

Deliverables

  • Source code
  • Application
  • Demo video
  • Technical report

PS3 — On-device threat, phishing & scam detection with full data privacy Theme: Cybersecurity & Digital Safety

Objective

Develop an on-device AI system that detects cybersecurity threats, including network intrusions, malware behaviour and phishing or scam communications, running entirely on Arm-based edge hardware without cloud dependency.

Project Description

Students build an AI-powered security assistant capable of:

  • Monitoring and classifying network traffic for anomalies and intrusions
  • Detecting phishing URLs
  • Detecting scam SMS messages or fraudulent communications
  • Explaining threats to non-expert users in plain language
  • Recommending remediation actions
  • Preserving privacy because no data leaves the device

Example Application Areas

  • Home and SME network intrusion detection
  • Scam call and SMS filtering
  • Phishing link detection
  • IoT device behaviour monitoring
  • Offline threat intelligence for air-gapped systems
  • Digital safety assistant for non-technical users

Hardware

  • Arm-based Android smartphones
  • Raspberry Pi 5
  • Raspberry Pi AI Kit (Hailo AI Accelerator)
  • Arm-based Linux devices

Required Technologies

  • Llama
  • ExecuTorch

Optional Technologies

  • CICIDS / UNSW-NB15 / NSL-KDD datasets
  • PhishTank / OpenPhish feeds
  • UCI SMS Spam Collection
  • Network packet capture libraries such as libpcap and Scapy
  • Hailo SDK

Deliverables

  • Source code
  • Application
  • Demo video
  • Technical report

  1. Plan approval
  2. Validated model
  3. Integrated prototype

  • Managed by IIT Delhi
  • Industry/academic mentor per team
